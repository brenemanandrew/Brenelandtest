# Peak Threads + Belinda's Spa, and Summit Slice (Andrew's designs, 2026-11-16)

Andrew's concept sheets: `concepts/andrew_peak_threads_design.jpg`, `concepts/andrew_summit_slice_design.jpg`.
Blender builds (first look): `blender/store_threads.py`, `blender/store_slice.py`, renders in `concepts/v2_*.png`.

## Setting
- Out of the strip mall: each store stands on its own, a little recessed, on a slightly raised spot with a path leading up to it.
- Mountain-village style: timber frame, cream plaster, stone, mossy roofs, hand-painted signs, flower pots.

## Peak Threads (ground floor) + Belinda's Spa (upstairs)
- Ground floor: fantasy apparel boutique (mannequins, gilt mirrors, shelves of bags and glasses, a table of folded stacks, the
  counter and till, a curved stair up).
- Upstairs: Belinda's Spa & Facial Modification Studio (spa chair, rugs, vanity, curtains, plants, lanterns, the "Facial Harmony &
  Modification Options" poster, a railing over the stair).
- Gameplay: the player sits or lies on the spa chair; a SPECIAL VERSION OF THE CHARACTER EDITOR opens with small "surgical" changes
  only (not a full re-make): head width, eye position, and a few other personal settings (nose, skin tone, hair regrowth per the
  poster). While it is up, the character can be shown down to the base model in undergarments with Belinda giving a massage.
  When the editor closes the character is updated, gets up and shakes Belinda's hand or high-fives her.

## Summit Slice (run by Zoe and Asha) + the girls' loft
- Day: traditional pizza place, kid-friendly (booths, counter and till, stone wood-fired oven, menu board, outdoor tables).
- Night: the Summit Lounge, adults only (21+): black light, neon, DJ booth, party lights. Not every night.
- The shop swaps to its party version about 15 minutes before a party starts and back about 15 minutes after it ends.
- Zoe and Asha live in the loft upstairs ("Loft entrance" door outside): they go up at the end of the night, come down at the start
  of the day, prep the store before it opens, then serve guests all day.
- NPCs visit during normal hours. A few regulars (anyone) go often on fixed days of their own; never packed, but party nights always
  have a good number of adults.
