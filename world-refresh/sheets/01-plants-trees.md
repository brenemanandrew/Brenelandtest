### Descriptions
- **T1 Oak:** A sturdy cartoon oak tree. Thick short brown trunk that flares at the roots, two or three chunky branches. Crown made of five big soft rounded leaf clumps, one large in the middle and smaller ones around it, like scoops of ice cream. Leaves mid green with lighter green tops and darker green undersides in a soft watercolour wash. A few simple dark painted bark lines. About 6 metres tall, crown wider than it is tall.
- **T2 Maple:** A round friendly cartoon maple tree. Medium brown trunk, slightly slimmer than an oak. Crown of four or five soft rounded leaf clumps forming a near ball. Warm green with yellow-green highlights, watercolour wash. About 5.5 metres tall.
- **T3 Birch:** A tall slim cartoon birch tree. Off-white trunk with a few short dark horizontal painted marks, gently curved. Narrow crown of three soft light green leaf clumps stacked upward. Airy and delicate but still chunky shapes. About 6.5 metres tall.
- **T4 Pine:** A cartoon pine tree made of four stacked rounded cone tiers, each tier with a soft scalloped lower edge, getting smaller toward a rounded tip. Short brown trunk visible at the bottom. Deep green with a lighter green rim on each tier, watercolour wash. About 7 metres tall.
- **T5 Fir:** A tall narrow cartoon spruce, five or six slim stacked tiers with drooping scalloped edges, blue-green and darker than a pine, lighter blue-green tier edges. Short dark trunk. About 9 metres tall.
- **T6 Palm:** A cartoon palm tree with a gently curved ringed trunk, thicker at the base, and seven broad drooping fronds like folded paper leaves. Bright green fronds with a lighter centre line, tan trunk with simple painted rings.
- **T7 Swing tree:** A grand old cartoon oak, extra thick trunk with big roots, one long strong horizontal limb reaching out to one side at about 3 metres height, wide crown of six or seven large soft leaf clumps. No swing, no rope.
- **Autumn versions (T1, T2, T3):** same prompt, replace the leaf colour line with: leaves in warm autumn orange, gold and rust red (birch: golden yellow), watercolour wash. Or make one version with pale neutral leaves so the game can tint it by season (preferred, see SWAP.md).
