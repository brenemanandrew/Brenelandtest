# Style guide

## The look in one line
A cozy small town, cel shaded, with simple Animal Crossing style shapes and flat colours that have a soft watercolour wash, like the concept sheet.

## What that means for a model
- Shapes: chunky, rounded, a little squat. Big readable silhouettes. Leaves are a few soft clumps, not thousands of leaf cards. No thin spikes, no noise.
- Colour: 2 to 4 flat colours per object, each with a gentle watercolour variation (a slightly lighter top, a slightly darker underside, soft blotches). No photo textures, no gloss, no metal shine, no baked hard shadows.
- Detail is drawn, not modelled: plank lines, bark marks, leaf veins as a few dark painted strokes, like ink on watercolour.
- Outline: the game draws its own dark ink outline. Do NOT ask Meshy for outlines on the model.
- Lighting: the game applies cel shading in bands. Textures must be flat lit (albedo only).
- Palette (from the concept sheet and the live game):
  - leaf greens #5daa45 #4f9a3e #6cb84c, pine #2f7a4a, fir #245e43
  - autumn #e8893a #d9602f #f0b33f #c9472d, birch gold #ecc94a
  - bark #7a5234, dark bark #6f4a2f, birch bark off-white with dark marks
  - wood plank browns #8a5a36 #6b4226, cream #f3ead8, brick red #b5452f, roof blue #3f5f8a, lamp glow #ffd98a
  - sheet palette: brown, red, green, yellow, blue, light blue, cream, navy

## The style block (paste at the start of EVERY Meshy prompt)
> Stylized low poly game asset, cozy cel shaded look, chunky rounded shapes like Animal Crossing, flat colours with a soft hand painted watercolour wash, simple painted details, matte, no gloss, no realistic texture, no outline, no ground, no base, single object centred, neutral flat lighting.

## Meshy settings
- Mode: text to 3D, or image to 3D from a crop of the concept sheet when the sheet shows the object.
- Topology: triangles, low poly. Use the triangle budget in each asset's row (remesh in Meshy to the target).
- Texture: base colour only, 1024 px (512 for small props).
- Pose and frame: upright, sitting on the ground plane, front facing the camera.
- Export: GLB.

## Checks before a model is accepted
1. Silhouette reads at a glance from 20 metres in game.
2. No more than 4 main colours.
3. Within the triangle budget.
4. Same footprint as the asset it replaces (see the sheet).
5. Looks at home next to the concept sheet.
