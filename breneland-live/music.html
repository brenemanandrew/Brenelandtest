Music

Our own music, made for Breneland and played live by the game on recorded instruments (a few notes of each, fetched when a song needs them). Each song is listed where it plays in the yard. Nothing plays until you tap Play. On an iPhone, turn the ringer switch on or the music stays silent.

Sound effects

The game's real sound effects, clips cut from open sound packs. Tap one to hear it (each go picks a clip and bends it a little, as the game does).

Dog voices

Every clip below is cut from a real dog recording. Tap Play to hear one, tap the heart on the ones you like, then Copy my picks and send the list back. The mood buttons play exactly what the game plays right now (a few clips bent to suit the mood), so tap them a few times: each go picks different clips. A coloured edge on a card means the game uses that clip today.
