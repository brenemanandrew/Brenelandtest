# Margo Vance (Little Shepersville story mode)

- `margo-base.glb`: Andrew's Hi3D base body (T-pose, white tank top and shorts, barefoot, no glasses). One still mesh, about 183k vertices, no skeleton, no face shapes yet. Glasses will be a separate accessory.
- `selfies/`: 16 pictures for rewards and messages, each with a caption and a use (chapter, checkin, milestone, warning, visit) in `margo.json`.
- `call/`: 8 head-and-shoulders frames for her video calls, by mood (warm, listening, skeptical, stern, delighted, smiling, evening, weekend).

Picked from Andrew's Hi3D renders (2026-11-27). Left out on purpose: the two bed-pose sheets and nightwear (the agent is motivating, not suggestive), and frames where her hair turned long or her look changed.
