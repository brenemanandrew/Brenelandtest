## Done
- New `vam/` folder in Brenelandtest: a VaM plugin (`vam/Custom/Scripts/Brene/Brene.cslist`) plus a relay on the PC (`vam/relay/brene-relay.mjs`, port 8767).
- Plugin: big themed panels (Scene, People, Glasses, Layout, Wizra, dock), 5 themes, size slider, an edit mode to drag panels, resize them, move buttons between panels and hide panels; layout saved to `Saves/PluginData/Brene/layout.json`.
- Glasses in VaM desktop mode: head tracking on the desktop camera (3DOF, 6DOF with `--6dof`), side-by-side 3D (Half and Full), re-centre, axis flips, eye spacing.
- Hands: relay page `/hands` (webcam, MediaPipe) sends hand positions; close the hand or pinch over a control point to hold and move it.
- Wizra chat in VaM: through brenai.fly.dev with her save (env `BRENAI_PIN`, `BRENAI_PASSWORD` on the PC) or LM Studio with her persona.
- Relay fix found by the test: it now reconnects when started before the glasses helper.
